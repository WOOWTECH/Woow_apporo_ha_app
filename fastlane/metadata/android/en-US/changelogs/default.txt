First release of Apporo aiot.

- Connect to an Apporo AP-1004S or AP-1005S controller commissioned by your integrator
- Open the dashboards configured on the controller and operate lighting, air conditioning, blinds and scenes
- Receive the alerts the controller raises
- Put a scene, switch or sensor reading on your home screen as a widget
- Tap an NFC tag to trigger a controller action
- Optionally report phone battery and connectivity back to the controller
